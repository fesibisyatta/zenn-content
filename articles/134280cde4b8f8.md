## 演習問題2.2

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[a_1 Y_1 + a_2 Y_2] &= a_1 \mu_1 + a_2 \mu_2 \\
\operatorname{Var}[a_1 Y_1 + a_2 Y_2] &= a_1 \sigma^2_1 + 2 a_1 a_2 \operatorname{Cov}(Y_1, Y_2) + a_2 \sigma^2_2 = a_1 \sigma_1^2 + a_2 \sigma_2^2\\
E[a_1 Y_1 - a_2 Y_2] &= a_1 \mu_1 - a_2 \mu_2 \\
\operatorname{Var}[a_1 Y_1 - a_2 Y_2] &= a_1 \sigma^2_1 - 2 a_1 a_2 \operatorname{Cov}(Y_1, Y_2) + a_2 \sigma^2_2 = a_1 \sigma_1^2 + a_2 \sigma_2^2\\
\end{aligned}
$$
